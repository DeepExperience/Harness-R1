The failure pattern is that the agent sometimes reaches a product detail page and clicks Buy Now even though the item is over the stated budget or required variants such as color, size, or material have not actually been selected. In WebShop this is especially harmful because Buy Now is the final purchase action, so the harness should intervene only at that high-risk moment rather than broadly steering navigation.

The patch adds an on_before_action hook that inspects only click actions whose normalized value is Buy Now. It then maps observable runtime signals to safety checks: the product_price_over_budget predicate and the current_price versus price_max state fields determine whether the item exceeds budget, while required_options_unselected indicates that a required variant is still missing. If the agent is trying to buy an over-budget item, the hook blocks the purchase and prompts it to return to search or choose an in-budget variant. If required options are unselected, it looks at the task’s required color, size, and material fields, compares them against the current clickable option labels, and rewrites the Buy Now click into a click on the matching visible option when one can be identified. This keeps the agent from completing an invalid purchase while still allowing normal browsing when the action is not Buy Now.
