The failure pattern is that the agent sometimes reaches a product detail page and tries to click Buy Now even though the item is not actually purchasable under the task constraints: either its current price is over the stated budget, or a required variant such as color, size, or material has not been selected yet. In WebShop this can turn an otherwise recoverable browsing step into an invalid purchase attempt, so the harness should intervene at the moment the Buy Now click is about to be taken rather than after the action has already been submitted.

The hook runs in on_before_action and only inspects click actions whose normalized value is Buy Now. It maps runtime predicates directly to blocking prompts: if product_price_over_budget is true, it blocks and tells the agent to return to search or results for an in-budget item; if required_options_unselected is true, it blocks and prompts the agent to choose the matching visible option or abandon the product if no matching option is available. This keeps normal search and selection behavior unchanged, but prevents the specific premature or invalid purchase action that the observed failures show.
