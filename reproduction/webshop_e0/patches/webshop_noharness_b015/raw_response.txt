The failure pattern is that the agent sometimes reaches a product detail page and tries to buy the item even though the page still has a required variant choice unselected, such as color, size, material, flavor, or count. In WebShop this can cause an invalid purchase attempt or prevent the model from completing the task, because the correct item has not actually been configured yet. The harness should intervene at the moment the agent attempts to click Buy Now, since that is the point where an otherwise recoverable configuration step is being skipped.

The hook runs in on_before_action and only inspects click actions whose normalized value is Buy Now. It maps the observable runtime predicate required_options_unselected to a block_and_prompt response, telling the agent to choose the matching visible option before buying. If no such option is available or the predicate is not set, the action is left unchanged. This keeps normal browsing and purchasing behavior intact while preventing the specific premature purchase error that occurs when required product options are still unset.
