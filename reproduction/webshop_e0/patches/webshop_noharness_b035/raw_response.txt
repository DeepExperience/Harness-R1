The failure pattern is that the agent sometimes reaches a product detail page and tries to buy the item even though the page still has a required variant choice unselected, such as color, size, material, flavor, or similar option. In WebShop this can cause an invalid purchase attempt or prevent the model from following the instruction to pick the matching variant before buying. The harness should intervene at the moment the model attempts to click Buy Now, because that is the point where an otherwise recoverable option-selection mistake becomes a failed action.

The hook runs in on_before_action and only inspects click actions whose normalized value is Buy Now. It maps the runtime predicate required_options_unselected to a block_and_prompt response, telling the agent to choose the matching visible option first or return to search if no matching option is available. This keeps normal browsing and option selection behavior unchanged, but prevents premature purchase when the current product page still has an unmet variant requirement.
