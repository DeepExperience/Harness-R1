The failure pattern is that the agent sometimes reaches a product detail page and tries to buy the item even though the page still has a required variant choice unselected, such as color, size, material, flavor, style, or length. In WebShop this can cause an invalid purchase attempt or prevent the model from completing the task, because the correct item is not actually chosen yet. The harness should intervene at the moment the model attempts to click Buy Now, since that is the point where an otherwise recoverable option-selection mistake becomes a failed purchase action.

The hook runs in on_before_action and only inspects click actions whose normalized value is Buy Now. It maps the runtime predicate required_options_unselected to a block_and_prompt response, telling the agent to choose the matching visible option before buying. If no matching option is visible, it directs the agent back to search instead of allowing an invalid purchase. This keeps normal browsing and option selection behavior unchanged, and only blocks the specific premature Buy Now action that the observable predicate indicates is not yet valid.
